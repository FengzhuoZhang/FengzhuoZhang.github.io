About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Center for Algorithms, Data, and Market Design at Yale, mentored by Dirk Bergemann and Zhuoran Yang. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National University of Singapore in 2025, advised by Vincent Y. F. Tan and Lin Zhao, and my Bachelor of Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2020.

My research interest is statistical and optimization theory for foundation models. My recent publications study optimization and training dynamics of language models, in-context learning and chain-of-thought reasoning, efficient long-context and speculative inference, and the economic design of personalized LLM services.
