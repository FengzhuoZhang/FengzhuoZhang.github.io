About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Center for Algorithms, Data, and Market Design at Yale University, where I am fortunate to be mentored by Professors Zhuoran Yang and Dirk Bergemann. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National University of Singapore in 2025 under the guidance of Professors Vincent Y. F. Tan and Lin Zhao. Prior to that, I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2020, where I had the privilege of working with Professor Yuan Shen.

My research interests lie in the statistical and optimization theory of foundation models. My recent work studies the optimization and training dynamics of language models, statistical models of In-Context Learning and Chain-of-Thought reasoning, and economic models of LLM services grounded in these optimization and statistical characterizations.

Research Highlights

01

Optimization of Foundation Models

I study how data distributions, optimizer geometry, and architecture shape foundation-model training, optimizer performance, and learned representations.

Selected work Associative Memory Curvature Perspective Feature Learning
02

Statistical Foundations of Inference and Reasoning

I develop statistical foundations of how language and multi-modal models use context and reasoning traces to make predictions, connecting in-context learning and chain-of-thought prompting to Bayesian inference.

Selected work In-Context Learning Chain-of-Thoughts Auto-Regressive Video Diffusions Auto-Regressive Image Generation
03

Game-Theoretic and Economic Foundations of AI Deployment

I analyze equilibria among heterogeneous users to understand how model performance and computational costs shape AI personalization, congestion, and platform design.

Selected work LLM Personalization Graphon Games
