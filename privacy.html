Privacy

This website uses privacy-preserving, first-party analytics to understand general visit patterns.

What is collected

For each page view, the site records the page, date, country, region, and a coordinate rounded to roughly one degree. The hosting service supplies this approximate location from the request before the page is served.

What is not collected

Raw IP addresses are never stored. The site does not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or third-party analytics scripts. A one-way daily identifier is used only to estimate visitor-days and cannot be used to recover an address.

Retention and access

Detailed analytics records are retained for up to 180 days. The statistics dashboard is restricted to the site owner and is not linked from the public navigation.

Your preference

Visits are not recorded when a browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Questions may be sent to fzzhang [at] u.nus.edu.
